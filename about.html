About this place

A notebook for useful curiosity.

I’m Akashdeep — an engineering student with an unreasonable interest in how the world fits together.

This is where I write my way toward better questions. I’m interested in science, technology, business, and the ideas that sit between them. Expect essays, reading notes, small experiments, and occasional uncertainty.

The goal isn’t to sound certain. It’s to notice clearly, think carefully, and share what I learn along the way.
